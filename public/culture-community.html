Signature program

Congressional Mongolian Lunar New Year

Each year, NNI brings Tsagaan Sar, the Mongolian Lunar New Year, to Capitol Hill — gathering community members, congressional and federal staff, diplomats, and civic partners for traditional art, performance, and Mongolian food.

Young leaders from NNI’s Youth Civic Leadership Program help shape the event and share how they researched issues affecting their community and turned their findings into policy priorities.
